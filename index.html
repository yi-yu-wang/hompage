About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Information and Library Science at Tamkang University.

I completed my Ph.D. program in Library and Information Science from National Taiwan University. My core research interests lie in improving information service quality and exploring issues related to information behavior and literacy. This focus is reflected in my previous work on archival users' expertise and the quality evaluation of mega-journals.
